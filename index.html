03 / DAKSHIN BHARAT HINDI PRACHAR SABHA

Examination hierarchy.

9-stage academic ladder with central government and university equivalence.

Tier 01 / 04 · Foundation Stage

Foundation Stage

(प्रारंभिक स्तर)

Alphabet, phonetics, and essential vocabulary.

Primary & Middle Equivalence
STAGE 01 परिचय
Parichaya
Basic Hindi Knowledge

Devanagari alphabet (स्वर & व्यंजन), phonetic sounds, vowel signs (मात्राएँ), and basic words.

Foundation Entry Level
STAGE 02 प्राथमिक
Prathamik
Elementary Proficiency

Sentence formation, basic nouns/verbs, short reading passages, and elementary grammar.

Elementary Primary Level
STAGE 03 मध्यमा
Madhyama
Intermediate Skills

Applied grammar (gender, number, tense, case), standard reading, and simple letter drafting.

Intermediate Middle School
Tier 02 / 04 · Secondary Stage

Secondary Stage

(माध्यमिक स्तर)

High school language competence leading to 10th Standard Hindi equivalence.

★ SSLC / Class 10 Hindi Equivalent
STAGE 04 राष्ट्रभाषा
Rashtrabhasha
National Language Proficiency

Textbook prose, selected poetry, social essays, précis writing, and conversational confidence.

Secondary Pre-Matric
STAGE 05 प्रवेशिका
Praveshika
Entrance to Higher Studies

Classical literature, advanced Sanskrit-Hindi grammar, and Hindi-English translation.

★ SSLC / 10th Equivalent Govt. Recognized
Tier 03 / 04 · Degree Level

Rashtrabhasha Visharad

(राष्ट्रभाषा विशारद)

Two-part literary scholarship recognized as equivalent to B.A. Hindi.

★ Bachelor of Arts (B.A. Hindi) Equivalent
STAGE 06 विशारद पूर्वार्ध
Visharad Poorvardh
Advanced Scholarship (Part 1)

Medieval poetry (Surdas, Tulsidas, Kabir), drama, and history of Hindi literature.

Degree Part 1 Paper I & II
STAGE 07 विशारद उत्तरार्ध
Visharad Uttarardh
Advanced Scholarship (Part 2)

Modern Hindi prose, literary essays, critical analysis, and advanced translation.

★ B.A. Degree Complete Govt. Validated
Tier 04 / 04 · Post-Graduate Level

Rashtrabhasha Praveen

(राष्ट्रभाषा प्रवीण)

Master-level qualification recognized as equivalent to M.A. Hindi.

★ Master of Arts (M.A. Hindi) Equivalent
STAGE 08 प्रवीण पूर्वार्ध
Praveen Poorvardh
Hindi Proficiency (Part 1)

Historical linguistics (भाषा विज्ञान), Hindi dialects, classical epics, and poetics.

Post-Grad Part 1 Paper I & II
STAGE 09 प्रवीण उत्तरार्ध
Praveen Uttarardh
Hindi Proficiency (Part 2)

Contemporary literary criticism, media journalism, research, and technical translation.
