02 / CURRICULUM

Academic programs.

Comprehensive academic support designed for school students and language learners, with flexible subject-specific scheduling.

Program 01 · School Tuition

CBSE & State Board Subject Tuition

Class 1 to 10 · Flexible & Need-Based Scheduling

We provide structured, individualized subject tuition for school students following both CBSE and State Board curricula from Class 1 through Class 10. Rather than enforcing a rigid one-size-fits-all schedule, classes are organized flexibly around each student's specific academic needs, school syllabus, and upcoming examination calendar.

Whether a student requires comprehensive guidance across all core subjects—such as Mathematics, Science, Social Science, and Languages—or focused reinforcement in a single challenging area, our faculty provides clear concept explanations, regular doubt-clearing, and systematic test series aligned with their school exams.

Curriculum: CBSE & State Board Format: Need-Based Scheduling
Program 02 · Certified Examination Ladder

Hindi Prachar Sabha Coaching

All 9 Stages: Parichaya to Praveen · Feb & Aug Sessions

Upadhi Tuition Center offers comprehensive, stage-by-stage coaching for all nine progressive examinations conducted by Dakshin Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha (Madras). From foundational literacy in Parichaya through post-graduate scholarship in Praveen, students receive dedicated preparation tailored for the biannual February and August examination sessions.

Coaching covers the complete official curriculum—including Devanagari phonetics, applied grammar rules, classical and modern prose, poetry, and bidirectional translation—culminating in government-recognized credentials equivalent to SSLC (Praveshika), B.A. Hindi (Visharad), and M.A. Hindi (Praveen).

Sessions: February & August Equivalence: SSLC, B.A. & M.A.
Program 03 · Language Studies

English Language Tuition

Class 1 to 12 · Grammar, Composition & Speaking

Our English tuition classes focus on building strong conceptual foundations in grammar, active vocabulary, reading comprehension, and formal writing. Designed for school students seeking academic excellence as well as learners developing conversational confidence, sessions integrate sentence structure analysis, textbook prose and poetry study, and guided speaking practice.

Students learn to articulate ideas clearly, eliminate common grammatical errors, and approach school essay writing and reading comprehension papers with ease and confidence.

Scope: Grammar & Literature Focus: Academic & Conversational
Program 04 · Regional Language

Malayalam Language Tuition

KG through Class 12 · Reading, Writing & Literature

Covering students from kindergarten through Class 12, our Malayalam language coaching supports both complete beginners learning the script and students following State Board or CBSE school syllabi. Instruction progresses systematically from Aksharamala letter recognition and correct pronunciation through applied grammar, textbook prose, poetry appreciation, and formal composition.

Individualized attention ensures that students overcome writing and spelling difficulties (chillaksharangal and kootaksharangal) while developing an authentic appreciation for Malayalam literature.

Grades: KG to Class 12 Boards: State & CBSE Support
03 / DAKSHIN BHARAT HINDI PRACHAR SABHA

Examination hierarchy.

Dakshin Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha (Madras) examinations follow a structured multi-tier academic ladder recognized by the Central Government and State Education Boards. We provide tailored coaching across all 9 progressive stages.

Tier 01 / 04 · Foundation Stage

Foundation Stage

(प्रारंभिक स्तर)

Elementary Hindi script, phonetic pronunciation, vocabulary, and basic conversational communication.

Primary & Middle Equivalence
STAGE 01 परिचय
Parichaya
Basic Hindi Knowledge

Introduction to Devanagari alphabet (स्वर & व्यंजन), correct pronunciation, vowel signs (मात्राएँ), and everyday vocabulary.

Foundation Entry Level
STAGE 02 प्राथमिक
Prathamik
Elementary Hindi Proficiency

Simple sentence construction, basic noun/verb forms, elementary comprehension passages, short stories, and foundational grammar rules.

Elementary Primary Level
STAGE 03 मध्यमा
Madhyama
Intermediate Hindi Skills

Applied grammar (लिंग, वचन, काल, कारक), conversational practice, reading standard prose, and informal letter drafting.

Intermediate Middle School
Tier 02 / 04 · Secondary & Matriculation Stage

Secondary Stage

(माध्यमिक स्तर)

High school language competence leading to official State Board SSLC / 10th Standard Hindi equivalence.

★ SSLC / Class 10 Hindi Equivalent
STAGE 04 राष्ट्रभाषा
Rashtrabhasha
National Language Proficiency

Textbook prose and selected poetry, essay composition on social themes, précis writing, and conversational confidence.

Secondary Pre-Matric
STAGE 05 प्रवेशिका
Praveshika
Entrance to Higher Hindi Studies

Classical and modern Hindi literature, comprehensive Sanskrit-Hindi grammar, and Hindi-English-Regional translation exercises.

★ SSLC / 10th Equivalent Govt. Recognized
Tier 03 / 04 · Degree Level

Rashtrabhasha Visharad

(राष्ट्रभाषा विशारद)

Advanced literary scholarship and translation studies, officially recognized as equivalent to a Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) in Hindi.

★ Bachelor of Arts (B.A. Hindi) Equivalent
STAGE 06 विशारद पूर्वार्ध
Visharad Poorvardh
Advanced Scholarship (Part 1)

Ancient & medieval poetry (Surdas, Tulsidas, Kabir), dramatic works, and an in-depth chronological history of Hindi literature (साहित्य का इतिहास).

Degree Part 1 Paper I & II
STAGE 07 विशारद उत्तरार्ध
Visharad Uttarardh
Advanced Scholarship (Part 2)

Modern Hindi prose, literary essays, critical analysis, comparative grammar, and advanced bidirectional translation studies.

★ B.A. Degree Equivalent Complete
Tier 04 / 04 · Post-Graduate Level

Rashtrabhasha Praveen

(राष्ट्रभाषा प्रवीण)

Master level proficiency for aspiring Hindi lecturers, teachers, and official translators, equivalent to Master of Arts (M.A.) in Hindi.

★ Master of Arts (M.A. Hindi) Equivalent
STAGE 08 प्रवीण पूर्वार्ध
Praveen Poorvardh
Hindi Proficiency (Part 1)

Historical linguistics (भाषा विज्ञान), evolution of Hindi dialects, classical epics (कामायनी, साकेत), and ancient poetics (काव्यशास्त्र).

Post-Grad Part 1 Paper I & II
STAGE 09 प्रवीण उत्तरार्ध
Praveen Uttarardh
Hindi Proficiency (Part 2)

Modern literary criticism, media journalism, research methodology, technical terminology, and professional administrative translation.

★ M.A. & Teacher Qualified Apex Level
